## 19. Common Mistakes to Avoid

❌ **DON'T:**
- Add keywords to business name ("Urban Morph | Sustainable Mobility Consultant")
- Use P.O. Box instead of physical address
- Create multiple profiles (Google will suspend)
- Ask for reviews in exchange for discounts (against policy)
- Use stock photos (use real team/project photos)
- Ignore negative reviews
- Let profile go inactive (post regularly)
- Use low-quality photos
- Spam with daily posts
- Copy-paste generic content

✅ **DO:**
- Keep information 100% accurate
- Use authentic photos
- Respond to all reviews
- Post valuable content regularly
- Update services and description
- Monitor insights monthly
- Build reviews organically
- Show personality in posts
- Highlight unique differentiators
